U.S. Steels Decline Explains Why You Need to Adopt AI

Odd right? What possibly could the decline of an industry over 50 years agos teach people today about the need to adpot AI.  Plenty.  A Bethlehem engineer in the 1980s succinctly described the decline this way; "We were making evolutionary changes when a technological revolution was going on."  Most of this post's observations come from the outstanding book "Crisis in Bethlehem." [1]. Anyone who has interest in technology, labor, tariffs, industry, pretty much every aspect of how we live today ought to give it a read.  It documents the downfall of Bethlehem Stell with deailed accounts of decline from post-WWII through 1986.
