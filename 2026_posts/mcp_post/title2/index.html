This post focuses upon Title II accessibility and novel and computationally repeatable applications of AI to solve them. It was inspired by my UB CATT training and Angela Thering, an accessibilty pro who taught me the ropes. A requirement for all Universitis is an improved offering of content that is Title II compliant. Compliance takes course content and provides accessibility hooks. For example, a PDF file will have a hidden set of tags that reflect the document's model like its sections, figures, appendicies. The tags are the guide posts for accesibility tools - they explain the model and often, the text that describes a figure, an equation, or a table. Critical content make course material accessible to all. A challenge is the sheer volume of document types. Word files, Powerpoint of Keynote, and, especially, the PDF. The PDF is at its heart focused on visual presentation of content. The addition of tags to provide accessibility is not native to the format. Many of tag issues are being solved by commercial companies like Adobe and Microsoft. But what about the LaTeX community, how have they proceeded. For those unfamiliar with LaTeX, here is a brief primer; think HTML markup focused on producing PDFs for journal or conference submissions. The LaTeX community has banded together to solve many of the tagging accessibility. One only needs consult to the tagpdf project to see the monumental effort by dedicated individuals to supply most of what the LaTeX community needs to generate Title II compliant document. However, one problem remains, a particular for LaTeX PDF generate called beamer. Think of it as Powerpoint or Keynote with many nicities geared to the academic community. There is simply no support team working to make the beamer format compliant; it is too hard. Imagine you are a professor with dozens or 100s of LaTeX beamer presenations. You options are not promising. You may switch to another format supported by LaTeX called ltktalk or switch entirely to another typesetting environment called typst. Neither has a silver bullet solution and requires a great deal of manual effort. Here, certainly, is an opprotunity for AI and LLMs to come to the resuce. A legitimate, real, and useful opportunity!
