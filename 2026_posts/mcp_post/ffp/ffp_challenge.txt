How the Government Could Use FFP to Spur True Agentic AI Innovation

Odd right?  A firm fixed price contract could be the catalyst to spur agentic AI growth.  It certainly could (and probably will!). Here is how.

The U.S. government has long procured software with a variety of contract vehicle.  Cost plus fixed fee, time and materials, direct purchase, and firm fixed price.   A neat experiment would be the following.  It could literally lead to a switch from the request for proposal model (tell us what you can do at your price) to firm fixed price (here is what we want, exactly, specifically at this set price).
