Chat Agents: Unwitting Guides in a Quest for Knowledge

This blog was inspired by all the folks who use chat agents the incorrect way when learning a concept.  Or better, the dangerous way.  One should use LLMs and chat agents warily.  They should be a last resort, consulted only after a person has tried to learn as much as possible indepedendently.  Develop a decent mental model first, then consult the chat agent to refine or expand points.
